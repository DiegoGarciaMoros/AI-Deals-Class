# Discovery interviews

Goal: learn how people track post-closing obligations today, and whether the
pain is big enough to pay for. **Do not pitch until the last five minutes.**
Ask about the past, not hypotheticals: what did you do on your last deal?

## Who to talk to (target 8, plus 2–3 controls)

| Segment | Roles | Why | Where to find them |
|---|---|---|---|
| PE funds (priority) | Deal team VP/Principal, portfolio operations, fund GC or CFO, fund controller | They own earnouts, escrows and claims across many portfolio companies | NYU Law and Stern alumni on LinkedIn; AI Deals class guest speakers; PE clubs |
| Corporate development at serial acquirers | Corp dev director, deputy GC for M&A | Several deals a year, lean legal teams | Alumni; ACG chapter events |
| Sellers' representatives and escrow agents | Shareholder representative services, bank escrow officers | They see how often obligations are missed or disputed | Industry panels; LinkedIn |
| M&A associates (2–3 max) | Mid-level associates | How post-closing work is handed off; do not discuss firm or client matters | Classmates' summer contacts |
| Controls for option #1 | Law firm risk, knowledge management or innovation staff | Check that AI governance isn't clearly the better idea | Law school career events; ILTA and CLOC contacts |

Professors in the AI Deals class are the best first intros. Ask each person at
the end: "Who else should I talk to?"

>
> Hi [Name], I'm a law student at NYU researching how deal teams track
> post-closing obligations like earnouts, escrow releases and indemnity

> connection] suggested you'd have a useful view.

## Script (20–25 minutes)

**Context (2 min)**
1. What's your role, and how many deals have you been on in the last two years?

**Current process (10 min)**
2. Walk me through the last deal that closed. After closing, who was responsible for tracking earnouts, escrow releases and survival deadlines?
3. Where does that tracking live? (Spreadsheet, calendar, outside counsel, memory?) Can you describe it?
4. How does it get built? Who reads the agreement, and how long does that take?
5. Across your portfolio or deal history, how many live post-closing items are there right now?

**Pain (8 min)**
6. Tell me about a time a deadline was missed, nearly missed, or disputed. What did it cost?
7. When an earnout or escrow dispute happened, how did you find the relevant terms and history?
8. What do you pay outside counsel for post-closing work today?
9. If this went away tomorrow, what would you stop worrying about?

**Solution check (last 5 min, only now show the demo)**
10. Show the demo. "What's wrong or missing?"
11. "Would you try this on a closed deal if we set it up for you?" A pilot yes is the real signal; "interesting" is not.
12. "Who else would need to approve using something like this?"

## Rules

- Never ask about or accept confidential client or deal information. Use only what they choose to share in general terms.
- Write notes within an hour. Record exact quotes about pain and money.
- Mark each interview: **Pilot yes / Maybe / No**.
