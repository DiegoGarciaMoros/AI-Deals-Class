The product

Upload the agreement. Get the docket.

1

Read

AI pulls every post-closing obligation out of the agreement, with the clause it comes from.

2

Compute

Code turns each rule, like "18 months after closing," into a date, and updates it when real dates come in.

3

Watch

One screen for the whole fund, with reminders 60 days before each deadline.

4

Record

Stamp what happened: paid, released, disputed or claimed. The history is ready when a dispute starts.
