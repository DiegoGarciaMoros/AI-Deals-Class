# Postclose

**The deal closed. The obligations didn't.**

## Problem

Every acquisition leaves behind months or years of live obligations: earnout
statements and payments, escrow releases, purchase price true-ups, indemnity
survival deadlines, non-competes and insurance tails. They sit in a 100-page
agreement and get tracked in a spreadsheet, if at all. A claim filed one day
late is barred. An escrow that isn't claimed in time is released. An earnout
dispute window that closes quietly makes the numbers final.

## Product

Upload the signed agreement. Postclose:

- **Extracts every post-closing obligation** with its trigger, amount, parties and the exact clause it comes from
- **Computes the deadlines** from the agreement's own rules (closing + 18 months, fiscal year end + 90 days, 10 business days after final determination) and recalculates when real dates come in
- **Flags what needs attention**: upcoming deadlines, disputes and open claims across a whole portfolio
- **Records what happened**: paid, disputed, claimed, released, so the history is ready when a dispute starts

Every item links to its source quote, so a lawyer can check it in seconds.

## Who it's for

PE funds and serial acquirers managing obligations across many closed deals,
and the lawyers who support them.
