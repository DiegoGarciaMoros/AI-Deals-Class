# Postclose

**Post-closing intelligence for PE funds and corporate development teams.**
The system of record for what a closed deal still requires.

## Problem

Every acquisition leaves behind years of live obligations: price adjustment
true-ups, earnout statements and payments, escrow releases, indemnity survival
deadlines, non-competes and insurance tails. They sit in a 100-page agreement
and get tracked in a spreadsheet, if at all.

The deadlines are unforgiving. A claim filed one day after the survival date is
barred. Escrow that isn't claimed in time goes to the sellers. An earnout
statement nobody disputes in the window becomes final.

**The lawyers leave at closing. The fund stays for years.** The law firm's
engagement ends around closing, and hourly billing gives firms little reason to
automate it. The buyer or seller lives with the obligations, often across dozens
of deals at once.

## Product

Upload the signed agreement. Postclose:

1. **Reads** every post-closing obligation, with the exact clause it comes from
2. **Computes** the deadlines from the agreement's own rules (closing + 18 months,
   fiscal year end + 90 days) and recalculates when real dates come in
3. **Watches** every deal on one screen for the whole fund, with reminders 60 days
   before each deadline
4. **Records** what happened (paid, released, disputed, claimed), so the history
   is ready when a dispute starts

The AI stays behind the scenes. Nobody chats with it; they open the docket.
A reviewer approves each deal's docket before reminders go out.

## Who pays

PE funds (GC, CFO, controller, deal team, portfolio operations) and companies
that make several acquisitions a year. Price to test: **$10–30k per fund per
year**, covering every deal. Law firms come later, as a channel.

## Where it goes

| Stage | What it is |
|---|---|
| V1 (built) | Docket for one deal |
| V2 (pilots) | Fund docket with reminders and outcome history |
| V3 | Upstream: closing checklists and signing deliverables that roll into the docket |
| V4 | The data layer for M&A: which terms were agreed, and which actually paid out |

## How it's different

Closing tools (Litera Transact) stop at closing. AI assistants (Harvey, Legora)
answer questions but don't keep a dated record. Contract management tools
(Ironclad) aren't built for earnouts and escrows. PE CRM and monitoring tools
(DealCloud, Chronograph) track pipelines and metrics, and Postclose can feed
them. The real incumbent is Excel plus a paralegal.
