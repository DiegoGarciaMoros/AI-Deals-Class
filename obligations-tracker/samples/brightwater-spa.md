## ARTICLE II — PURCHASE AND SALE

**Section 2.1 Purchase and Sale.** Upon the terms and subject to the conditions of this Agreement, at the Closing, the Sellers shall sell to Buyer, and Buyer shall purchase from the Sellers, all of the issued and outstanding Shares.

**Section 2.3 Escrow Deposits.** At the Closing, Buyer shall deposit with Harbor National Trust Company (the "Escrow Agent") (a) $1,500,000 (the "Adjustment Escrow Amount") and (b) $9,000,000 (the "Indemnity Escrow Amount"), each to be held and disbursed in accordance with this Agreement and the Escrow Agreement.

**Section 2.4 Purchase Price Adjustment.**

(a) Within ninety (90) days after the Closing Date, Buyer shall prepare and deliver to the Sellers' Representative a statement (the "Closing Statement") setting forth Buyer's good faith calculation of Closing Working Capital, Closing Cash and Closing Indebtedness.

(b) The Sellers' Representative shall have forty-five (45) days after delivery of the Closing Statement (the "Review Period") to deliver to Buyer a written notice of objection (an "Objection Notice"). If the Sellers' Representative does not deliver an Objection Notice prior to the expiration of the Review Period, the Closing Statement shall be final and binding on the parties.

(c) Within five (5) Business Days after the final determination of the Closing Statement, (i) if the Final Purchase Price is less than the Estimated Purchase Price, Buyer and the Sellers' Representative shall jointly instruct the Escrow Agent to release to Buyer from the Adjustment Escrow Amount the amount of such shortfall, and (ii) the Escrow Agent shall release the remaining Adjustment Escrow Amount to the Sellers.

**Section 2.5 Indemnity Escrow Release.**

(a) On the date that is twelve (12) months after the Closing Date, the Escrow Agent shall release to the Sellers fifty percent (50%) of the Indemnity Escrow Amount, less the aggregate amount of all then-pending claims for indemnification made in good faith by Buyer.

(b) On the date that is eighteen (18) months after the Closing Date (the "Final Release Date"), the Escrow Agent shall release to the Sellers the remaining balance of the Indemnity Escrow Amount, less the aggregate amount of all then-pending claims for indemnification made in good faith by Buyer.

**Section 2.6 Earnout.**

(a) As additional consideration, Buyer shall pay to the Sellers (i) $10,000,000 if Net Revenue for Fiscal Year 2025 equals or exceeds $48,000,000 (the "2025 Earnout Payment") and (ii) $10,000,000 if Net Revenue for Fiscal Year 2026 equals or exceeds $58,000,000 (the "2026 Earnout Payment"). No partial Earnout Payment shall be payable.

(b) Within ninety (90) days after the end of each of Fiscal Year 2025 and Fiscal Year 2026, Buyer shall deliver to the Sellers' Representative a statement setting forth its calculation of Net Revenue for such Fiscal Year (each, an "Earnout Statement").

(c) The Sellers' Representative shall have thirty (30) days after delivery of an Earnout Statement to deliver a written notice of dispute. Any dispute not resolved by the parties within thirty (30) days after delivery of such notice shall be submitted to the Independent Accountant.

(d) Buyer shall pay any Earnout Payment that becomes payable within ten (10) Business Days after the final determination of Net Revenue for the applicable Fiscal Year.

## ARTICLE VI — COVENANTS

**Section 6.2 Restrictive Covenants.**

(a) For a period of five (5) years after the Closing Date, no Key Seller shall, directly or indirectly, engage in, own, manage or operate any business that competes with the Business anywhere in the United States or Canada.

(b) For a period of two (2) years after the Closing Date, no Seller shall, directly or indirectly, solicit for employment or hire any employee of the Company.

**Section 6.3 D&O Tail.** Prior to the Closing, the Company shall purchase a six (6)-year "tail" directors' and officers' liability insurance policy, and for six (6) years after the Closing Date, Buyer shall cause the Company not to cancel, amend or otherwise modify such policy.

**Section 6.4 Tax Refunds.** Buyer shall pay to the Sellers' Representative, for the benefit of the Sellers, the amount of any Pre-Closing Tax Refund received by Buyer or the Company, net of reasonable out-of-pocket costs, within fifteen (15) days after receipt thereof.

**Section 6.5 Retention Bonuses.** On the first anniversary of the Closing Date, Buyer shall cause the Company to pay retention bonuses in an aggregate amount of $2,000,000 to the Continuing Employees listed on Schedule 6.5 who remain employed by the Company on such date.

## ARTICLE VIII — INDEMNIFICATION

**Section 8.1 Survival.**

(a) The representations and warranties of the Company and the Sellers contained in this Agreement (other than the Fundamental Representations and the Tax Representations) shall survive the Closing until the date that is eighteen (18) months after the Closing Date.

(b) The Fundamental Representations shall survive the Closing until the date that is six (6) years after the Closing Date.

(c) The Tax Representations shall survive until sixty (60) days after the expiration of the applicable statute of limitations.

**Section 8.4 Claims Procedures.** Any Indemnified Party seeking indemnification shall deliver a written notice (a "Claim Notice") to the Indemnifying Party. The Indemnifying Party shall have thirty (30) days after receipt of a Claim Notice to deliver a written objection; if no objection is delivered within such period, the Indemnifying Party shall be deemed to have accepted the claim.

**Section 8.5 Limitations.** The Sellers shall not be liable under Section 8.2(a) unless and until the aggregate Losses exceed $900,000 (the "Deductible"), and then only for Losses in excess of the Deductible. The aggregate liability of the Sellers under Section 8.2(a) shall not exceed the Indemnity Escrow Amount.
